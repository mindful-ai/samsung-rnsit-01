# AI-Powered Research and Presentation Challenge

## Objective

In this hands-on workshop, you will use Generative AI to research a topic of your choice, organize the information, and create a professional presentation within a limited time.

The goal is to demonstrate how AI can accelerate research, content creation, and presentation development while maintaining accuracy and clarity.

---

# Scenario

You have been invited to deliver a **10-minute presentation** at a technology event. Unfortunately, you have **only 30 minutes** to prepare.

You must use AI tools effectively to:

1. Research your chosen topic.
2. Collect reliable information.
3. Organize the content logically.
4. Create presentation slides.
5. Prepare for a short presentation.

---

# Your Task

Choose one topic that interests you, such as:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Quantum Computing
- Space Exploration
- Electric Vehicles
- Climate Change
- Cybersecurity
- Cricket Analytics
- Blockchain
- Renewable Energy
- Your favorite hobby or technology

You may choose any topic appropriate for a professional audience.

---

# Deliverables

## 1. Research Summary

Include:

- Overview
- Key concepts
- Current trends
- Real-world applications
- Challenges
- Future outlook

## 2. Presentation

Create a 6–8 slide presentation with:

1. Title
2. Introduction
3. Key Concepts
4. Applications
5. Current Trends
6. Challenges
7. Future Scope
8. Conclusion

## 3. References

Include the sources used for your research.

---

# Constraints

- Total time: **30 minutes**
- Use AI to accelerate your work.
- Verify important facts.
- Keep slides concise with bullet points.
- Use visuals where appropriate.

---

# Suggested Workflow

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5 min | Select topic and define scope |
| 10 min | Research and collect information |
| 10 min | Create presentation slides |
| 5 min | Review and refine |
